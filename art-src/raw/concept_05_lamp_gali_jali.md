## Prompt

In-game screenshot of a 3D pixel-art story adventure set in a fantasy city inspired by Banaras (Varanasi). Camera: orthographic, elevated three-quarter view. A very narrow, dark lane only two metres wide between tall weathered plastered houses (faded blue and ochre walls, small wooden doors, a shrine niche with a single flame) dead-ends at a large carved stone jali screen. Through the screen's intricate geometric lattice pours intense warm orange light and drifting incense smoke from hundreds of oil lamps on the other side, throwing a lattice pattern of light onto the lane floor and walls. A hooded traveler in a deep indigo cloak and saffron scarf stands silhouetted before it. Mysterious, hushed. Rendering style: real 3D with realistic, physically based lighting but rendered at low resolution so everything is built from crisp, hard-edged pixels, with subtle dark outlines and gentle dithering, like a modern HD-2D game. Limited palette: deep indigo and violet darkness against blazing saffron light. No text, no UI, no watermark.
