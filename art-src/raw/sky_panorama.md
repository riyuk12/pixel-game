## Prompt

A seamless 360-degree panoramic backdrop strip for a video game skybox, painted as rich pixel art. A mythical holy city on a great river at the moment after sunset. The horizon sits in the lower third of the image. Along the horizon, far away and hazy, rise colossal impossible temple spires several times taller than any real building, golden domes, palaces stacked on palaces, towers linked by bridges, and on one stretch the open river with sandbanks and distant boats. Above, a deep gradient sky from glowing gold and rose at the horizon to violet and indigo overhead, a huge pale moon, the first stars, and hundreds of tiny glowing sky lanterns drifting upward. Soft volumetric haze and light rays. Wide, epic, mystical, Indian fantasy. The left and right edges must join seamlessly. No text, no frame, no watermark.
