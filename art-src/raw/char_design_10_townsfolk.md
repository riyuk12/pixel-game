## Prompt

Character design sheet for a 3D pixel-art story game set in a fantasy city inspired by Banaras: ten varied background townsfolk and pilgrims standing in a row at the same scale, full body, front three-quarter view, on a plain flat warm-grey background — an elderly pilgrim woman in a white sari, a boy holding a paper kite, a shopkeeper with a ledger, a brass-beater with a small hammer, a dyer with indigo-stained arms carrying dyed cloth, a woman carrying a brass water pot on her head, a tabla player, a young pilgrim couple, a man leading a cow on a rope, a girl carrying marigold garlands. Stylised proportions about six heads tall, clear silhouettes, designed to be built as low-poly 3D models. Crisp pixel art with soft warm key light, consistent in style with the attached character lineup. No text, no labels, no watermark.
