## Prompt

In-game screenshot of a 3D pixel-art story adventure set in a fantasy city inspired by Banaras (Varanasi). Camera: orthographic, elevated three-quarter view looking north across a stone forecourt at the top of the city towards an old Nagara-style temple with a tall, intricately carved shikhara spire. Rows of brass bells hang across the entrance; a small pavilion shelters a stone Nandi bull; pilgrims ring bells and carry offerings; oil lamps flicker on the steps. The last violet light of dusk behind the spire, the first stars, warm lamp-light below. Rendering style: real 3D with realistic, physically based lighting but rendered at low resolution so everything is built from crisp, hard-edged pixels, with subtle dark outlines and gentle dithering, like a modern HD-2D game. Rich but limited palette of saffron, marigold, sandstone gold, deep maroon, indigo and violet shadow. No text, no UI, no watermark.
