## Prompt

In-game screenshot of a 3D pixel-art story adventure set in a fantasy city inspired by Banaras (Varanasi). Camera: orthographic, elevated three-quarter view from the river looking north. An ornate, many-storeyed riverside palace rises straight from the ghat steps: stacked carved balconies, small domes and chhatris, rows of pointed arches, all in golden sandstone; at its base a dark vaulted tunnel entrance leads up into the city. On the steps below, sadhus in saffron sit under large bamboo umbrellas beside two small stone chhatri pavilions; boats are moored at the bottom step. Low golden sun raking across the facade, long shadows, warm haze over the water. Rendering style: real 3D with realistic, physically based lighting but rendered at low resolution so everything is built from crisp, hard-edged pixels, with subtle dark outlines and gentle dithering, like a modern HD-2D game. Rich but limited palette of saffron, marigold, sandstone gold, deep maroon, indigo and violet shadow. No text, no UI, no watermark.
