## Prompt

In-game screenshot of a 3D pixel-art story adventure set in a fantasy city inspired by Banaras (Varanasi) on the Ganga, with Rajasthani haveli architecture. Camera: orthographic, elevated three-quarter view from just above the river, looking north at the main ghat. A wooden boat carrying a hooded traveler in a deep indigo cloak and saffron scarf touches the wet bottom step. Five priests in maroon shawls and gold dhotis stand on small square stone platforms at the water's edge, lifting tall tiered brass aarti lamps that blaze with fire and smoke. Behind them broad stone ghat steps rise away from the camera, crowded with pilgrims, marigold garlands and bamboo umbrellas. At the top of the steps a continuous wall of tall carved golden-sandstone havelis with jharokha balconies; a wide central stairway lined with flower sellers climbs into the city; a small shikhara temple stands to its right; further right an ornate many-storeyed palace rises straight off the steps. Floating leaf-cup diyas on the dark water, boats moored along the bank. Dusk: low golden sun from the left, long soft shadows across the steps, violet sky glow, warm firelight and smoke haze. Rendering style: real 3D with realistic, physically based lighting but rendered at low resolution so everything is built from crisp, hard-edged pixels, with subtle dark outlines and gentle dithering in gradients, like a modern HD-2D game. Rich but limited palette of saffron, marigold, sandstone gold, deep maroon, indigo and violet shadow. No text, no UI, no watermark.
