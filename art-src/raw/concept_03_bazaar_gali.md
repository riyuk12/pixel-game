## Prompt

In-game screenshot of a 3D pixel-art story adventure set in a fantasy city inspired by Banaras (Varanasi), with Rajasthani haveli architecture. Camera: orthographic, elevated three-quarter view looking north along a narrow, crowded market lane about six metres wide, walled in by tall carved golden-sandstone havelis whose balconies overhang the lane. Stalls press in from both sides: mounds of red, yellow and green spices, stacked brass pots, hanging Banarasi silk saris, glass bangles, trays of sweets; cloth awnings and strings of small lamps overhead; a dense crowd and a cow in the middle of the lane. At the far end of the lane rises the tall carved stone spire of an old temple, catching the last sunlight. On the right, a narrow side opening between two stalls reveals a glimpse of a lamp-lit courtyard where a dancer spins. Dusk: lane in deep warm shadow, pools of lamp-light, golden light only on the highest stones. Rendering style: real 3D with realistic, physically based lighting but rendered at low resolution so everything is built from crisp, hard-edged pixels, with subtle dark outlines and gentle dithering, like a modern HD-2D game. Rich but limited palette of saffron, marigold, sandstone gold, deep maroon, indigo and violet shadow. No text, no UI, no watermark.
