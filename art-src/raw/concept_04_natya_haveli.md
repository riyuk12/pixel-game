## Prompt

In-game screenshot of a 3D pixel-art story adventure set in a fantasy city inspired by Banaras (Varanasi), with Rajasthani haveli architecture. Camera: orthographic, elevated three-quarter view. In the foreground a dim narrow lane ends at a carved pointed stone archway; through it we see into a haveli courtyard lit by oil lamps and hanging lanterns. A Kathak dancer in a flared red-and-gold costume spins on a small raised stone stage; tabla and sarangi players sit cross-legged on a Persian carpet beside her; an audience sits on carpets around the courtyard; onlookers lean out of carved jharokha balconies on the upper floors. Warm golden lamp-light inside the courtyard against cool violet dusk shadow in the lane. Rendering style: real 3D with realistic, physically based lighting but rendered at low resolution so everything is built from crisp, hard-edged pixels, with subtle dark outlines and gentle dithering, like a modern HD-2D game. Rich but limited palette of saffron, marigold, sandstone gold, deep maroon, indigo and violet shadow. No text, no UI, no watermark.
