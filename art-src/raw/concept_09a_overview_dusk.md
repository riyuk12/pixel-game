## Prompt

Among the attached images is a top-down layout plan drawn as flat coloured tiles on a grid, with a blue river strip along the bottom. Build exactly that layout as a 3D city district and show it from a high three-quarter aerial view, camera above the river looking north, so the whole district fits in frame. How to read the plan: the blue strip is the Ganga river with small boats; the beige horizontal bands above it are wide stone ghat steps climbing up from the water, with five small square aarti platforms at the water's edge in the middle; the chevron-marked strips are stairways climbing into the city (a wide central stairway lined with orange flower stalls, a narrow one on the left); each coloured block is an individual 2- to 6-storey house — the block colour is its plaster colour (blue-washed, ochre, rose, golden sandstone, whitewash) — with flat roof terraces, laundry and small domed chhatris; the light lanes between blocks are narrow galis; the large dark maroon blocks on the right are an ornate many-storeyed palace rising straight off the ghat steps with an arched tunnel beside it, and the dark block just right of the central stairway is a small shikhara temple; the square at the top left is the Courtyard of Lamps — a sunken stepwell with three tiers of steps down to a dark pool with a tiny island shrine, a giant banyan in its corner, an arcaded gallery around it, every step lined with glowing oil lamps and filled with smoke; the light square in the centre is a chowk with a peepal tree; the lane lined with orange squares running north from it is the bazaar; the light rectangle on the right inside a dark ring is a haveli courtyard with a small stage; the small courtyard at the far right with orange squares holds handlooms; the plaza at the top centre is a temple forecourt with a tall carved shikhara temple spire on the top edge. Architecture: Banaras ghats and Rajasthani havelis — carved golden sandstone, jharokha balconies, jali screens. Rendering style: real 3D with realistic, physically based lighting but rendered at low resolution so everything is built from crisp, hard-edged pixels, with subtle dark outlines and gentle dithering, like a modern HD-2D game diorama. No text, no labels, no grid lines, no UI, no watermark. Lighting: dusk, low golden sun from the left raking across the rooftops, long shadows, violet sky reflected in the river, the aarti fires burning at the water's edge, warm lamps beginning to glow in the lanes and the stepwell.
