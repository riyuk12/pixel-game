## Prompt

In-game screenshot of a 3D pixel-art story adventure set in a fantasy city inspired by Banaras (Varanasi), with Rajasthani haveli architecture. Camera: orthographic, elevated three-quarter view looking north up a wide stone stairway that climbs from the river ghats into the city. Both sides of the stairs are lined with garland sellers under small cloth awnings, heaped with mountains of orange marigold and red rose garlands; pilgrims climb the steps. At the top the stairs open onto a small square (chowk): a glowing chai stall with a kettle on a coal stove, a resting cow, a peepal tree with a tiny red shrine at its base, tall carved havelis all around with jharokha balconies and laundry on the rooftops. Dusk: last golden sunlight on the upper storeys, the stairs in cool violet shadow, warm lamp-light from the stalls. Rendering style: real 3D with realistic, physically based lighting but rendered at low resolution so everything is built from crisp, hard-edged pixels, with subtle dark outlines and gentle dithering, like a modern HD-2D game. Rich but limited palette of saffron, marigold, sandstone gold, deep maroon, indigo and violet shadow. No text, no UI, no watermark.
