## Prompt

In-game screenshot of a 3D pixel-art story adventure set in a fantasy city inspired by Banaras (Varanasi). Camera: orthographic, elevated three-quarter view looking down into the Courtyard of Lamps: a sunken stepwell (baori) inside a walled courtyard. Three square terraces of stone steps descend to a dark, still sacred pool with a tiny glowing shrine on an island at its centre. Thousands of small clay oil lamps line every step and every terrace; worshippers sit in rows on each level in prayer. A colossal ancient banyan tree grows from one corner of the courtyard, its aerial roots hanging down over the terraces into the water. Above, a U-shaped upper gallery with carved arches runs around three sides of the courtyard, where more figures hold lamps. Thick incense smoke fills the well and glows orange; the flames are reflected in the black water. Mystical, sacred, otherworldly; faint moonlight from above mixes with the firelight. Rendering style: real 3D with realistic, physically based lighting but rendered at low resolution so everything is built from crisp, hard-edged pixels, with subtle dark outlines and gentle dithering, like a modern HD-2D game. Limited palette of saffron, amber and gold firelight against deep indigo and violet shadow. No text, no UI, no watermark.
